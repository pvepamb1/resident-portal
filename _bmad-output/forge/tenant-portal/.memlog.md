- (decision) Scope: individual landlord-tenant management, NOT gated-community/society management (explicitly ruled out)
- (decision) Two-sided from the start: landlord side (add tenant info, track payments) + tenant side (pay rent, set reminders)
- (crack) 'Could sell to other landlords later' is aspirational, not scoped — building for one landlord (self) vs. building resellable multi-tenant SaaS pull the design in different directions; deferred, not resolved
- (decision) Payment flow = Option A: money moves through the app (real payment collection, not just record-keeping)
- (lock) POC architecture: build for single-landlord (self) use only; keep the payment module isolated/modular so productizing later means rewriting the payment layer only, not the whole app. Resolves earlier 'self vs. product' tension for now.
- (crack) Scope mismatch: actual current pain is 1 unit, 1 tenant, rent already via direct bank transfer, occasional late payment, wants a payment history. A two-sided app with payment-gateway integration is enormous relative to that problem — a shared sheet, a reminder, or a standing instruction would fix the stated pain in an afternoon. Real driver may be wanting to build/ship a real product (using tenant as first use case) rather than solving urgent personal pain.
- (lock) Real driver confirmed: this is a product-building goal, not urgent personal pain relief. Own tenancy (1 unit) is the first use case / dogfood, not the target problem. Design conversation should now center on what makes this valuable to OTHER landlords, not on the user's own single tenant.
- (decision) Business model shape: free tier for 1-2 unit landlords to build user base/proof; monetize later via additional features sold to larger multi-unit landlords
- (assumption) Unverified assumption: that a free user base of small/hobbyist landlords will read as credible 'proof' (stable, popular, capable) to larger multi-unit landlords deciding whether to pay — these are different buyers with different trust signals
- (decision) Target segment redefined: 1-5 unit landlords only (not large multi-unit portfolios/property managers). Large customers are an explicit stretch goal, not a design target — if scaling up requires expensive redesign, a separate new app would be built later, borrowing ideas, rather than reshaping this one.
- (crack) Superseded/walked back: earlier plan to monetize via free hobbyist base building credibility with LARGER landlords is dropped. Replacement claim: a large free user base is general publicity for 'a tool in the category' that could be leveraged to advertise a 'Plus' version — untested claim, and it's a different mechanism (marketing reach vs. credibility signal) that still needs its own scrutiny.
- (decision) Monetization for the 1-5 unit segment is explicitly undecided/deferred — not resting on an unexamined 'popularity will figure it out' plan. POC proceeds without a validated revenue model.
- (lock) Payment flow clarified: app is pure orchestration on a licensed gateway (Razorpay/Cashfree/etc.) — it never holds/custodies funds, so no RBI Payment Aggregator license burden falls on the app itself.
- (decision) TDS (Sec 194-IB) / tax handling explicitly OUT OF SCOPE for the POC. Noted risk: tenant's TDS obligation (rent >Rs.50k/month) is real and applies regardless of payment channel, and a meaningful slice of the 1-5 unit target segment (single flats in metros) will cross that threshold — deferred, not eliminated.
- (lock) Auto-pay is IN v1 (tenant side). Resolved concern: 'tap to confirm' for above-threshold UPI Autopay debits happens in the tenant's own UPI app (GPay/PhonePe/etc.) via NPCI push, not in the landlord's product — so a website (no native app) is sufficient; standard pattern already used by Netflix/gym/insurance recurring UPI mandates. Pre-build task: verify current UPI Autopay AFA threshold and mandate rules against chosen gateway's docs before implementation — user's own rent (Rs.25,000/month, Chennai) is confidently above the historical threshold, so this is a real UX branch to design for, not hypothetical.
- (decision) Reminders, payment history, and lease documents require no tenant install/enrollment (can ride a one-tap UPI payment link) — auto-pay is the only tenant-facing feature requiring active tenant enrollment (mandate setup).
- (note) Resumed session. Reopening TDS thread as next branch.
- (decision) TDS-in-auto-pay design concern deliberately deferred: mechanism is a once-a-year (or once-per-tenancy-end) lump deduction on the final payment, not a monthly reconciliation problem. Flagged for later, not designed now.

Downgraded to a backlog research task: check (a) whether e-filing can be automated at all given likely per-filing tenant OTP/authentication requirements, (b) re-litigate the tenant-vs-landlord beneficiary mismatch before ever committing to build. Tier A (calculate + adjust the once-a-year payment, no filing) remains the only TDS work actually on the roadmap.

Monetization model deferral reaffirmed (consistent with earlier lock): MVP/POC proceeds without a validated revenue model.

Initial cohort framed explicitly as beta testers; paid tier design (if any) will be shaped by their feedback, not decided upfront.
