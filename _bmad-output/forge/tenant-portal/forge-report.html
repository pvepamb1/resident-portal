Tenant & Rental Payment Portal

A landlord–tenant rent payment app for India's 1–5 unit landlords, forged from a single-tenant personal itch into a scoped, buildable POC.

Started as "manage my rent" for one flat in Chennai and a rent already moving by bank transfer. Under questioning, that turned out to be a product-building goal wearing a personal-pain costume — the real target became India's 1–5 unit landlords, with the user's own tenancy kept only as the dogfood case. What follows is what survived that questioning, what got cut, and what's still open on purpose.

What's locked

  1. Scope

    Individual landlord–tenant only — gated-community / society management explicitly ruled out.

  2. Build

    Single-landlord POC first, payment module kept isolated — productizing later means rewriting one layer, not the app.

  3. Driver

    This is a product-building goal, not personal pain relief — the user's own 1-tenant case is dogfood, not the target.

  4. Segment

    1–5 unit landlords only — large multi-unit portfolios are an explicit stretch goal, not a design constraint.

  5. Payments

    Pure orchestration on a licensed gateway (Razorpay/Cashfree-class) — the app never custodies funds, so no RBI Payment Aggregator license burden.

  6. Auto-pay

    UPI Autopay mandate ships in v1 — the tap-to-confirm step happens inside the tenant's own UPI app via NPCI push, so a website is enough.

  7. Tax

    TDS mechanics resolved: Sec 194-IB is a once-a-year lump deduction on the final payment, not monthly — a calculate-and-adjust feature is real but post-MVP.

  8. Money

    Free-tier guardrail: whoever is on the product when a paid tier launches stays free — no retroactive paywalling of early beta users.

What didn't survive

  1. Cut

    Gated-community / society management — different problem, different buyer, dropped at the door.

  2. Cut

    Free hobbyist base as a credibility signal to large landlords — large multi-unit landlords and small hobbyists are different buyers with different trust signals; the story didn't hold.

  3. Cut

    Large multi-unit portfolios as the primary target — demoted to stretch goal; a separate app gets built later if scaling ever demands it.

  4. Cut

    Auto-filing Form 26QC / issuing Form 16C on the tenant's behalf — the compliance relief lands on the tenant, not the paying landlord, and e-filing automation is unverified. Downgraded to a backlog research question.

Still open — carried forward on purpose

  1. Open

    Whether landlords other than the user feel this problem enough to switch off a spreadsheet — never tested. Accepted as something to learn by shipping, not prove in advance.

  2. Open

    "A free user base becomes publicity for a paid tier" — still an unproven mechanism, knowingly left unvalidated.

  3. Open

    UPI Autopay's current AFA threshold and mandate rules — needs verifying against the chosen gateway's actual docs before build, not just against memory of the rule.

  4. Open

    PAN capture and PAN–Aadhaar link validation for correct TDS withholding — noted, not designed; fine for now since it rides on the post-MVP tax work.

Who pressure-tested this

📊Mary— Business Analyst

Pushed past "out of scope for the POC" to ask what the payment-history data model silently assumed — first to name the gross-vs-net rent mismatch.

🧾Raghav— Tax CA (invented)

Walked the Sec 194-IB mechanics in real rupees — deduction timing, PAN penalties, and why "hassle-free filing" is a harder promise than it sounds.
